MY STORY

One person.
One bench.

401comp is my independent computer-help shop. It started with a two-year Computer Information Systems degree from New England Institute of Technology, where peer-tutoring coursework turned into a teaching offer at graduation — a new job's timing meant taking a different path instead.

From there came programmer/analyst work on healthcare systems, including MUMPS, and later a small business fixing other people's Windows machines.

These days, I bring that same hands-on, diagnosis-first approach to every problem. I’m not here to guess, upsell, or throw software at something until it shuts up. I’m here to find what’s actually wrong, fix it properly, and leave you with a clear answer and a product you can trust.
