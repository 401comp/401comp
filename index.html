ABOUT

One person.
One bench.

401comp is a one-person shop. It started with a two-year Computer Information Systems degree from New England Institute of Technology, where peer-tutoring coursework turned into a teaching offer at graduation — a new job's timing meant taking a different path instead.

From there came programmer/analyst work on healthcare systems, including MUMPS, and later a small business fixing other people's Windows machines.

The software here comes from the same instinct: find the actual problem, fix it properly, and make sure it's still fixed tomorrow.
